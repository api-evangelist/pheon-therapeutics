Pheon Therapeutics (Pheon), a clinical-stage leading Antibody-Drug Conjugate (ADC) specialist developing next-generation ADCs for a wide range of hard-to-treat cancers, today announced the appointment of accomplished oncology expert and biotech innovator, Samuel Blackman, MD, PhD, as an independent, Non-Executive Director to its Board.
