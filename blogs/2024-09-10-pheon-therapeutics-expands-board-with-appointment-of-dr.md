Pheon Therapeutics (Pheon), a leading Antibody-Drug Conjugate (ADC) specialist developing next generation ADCs for a wide range of hard-to-treat cancers, today announces the appointment of accomplished biotech executive, Enoch Kariuki, Pharm.D., to its Board of Directors as an independent, non-executive director.
